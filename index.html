EQUISPLIT / THE METHOD

Fair splits.
Down to the last paise.

EquiSplit works out each share in your browser, keeps the arithmetic exact, and shows who owes what. No complicated setup—just enter a bill and your group size.

01 / INPUT

Enter the details

Add an optional occasion name, the total amount, and how many people are sharing. Choose your currency from the calculator.

02 / CALCULATE

Divide without drift

The total is converted to the currency's smallest units before division. That avoids floating-point rounding surprises.

03 / SETTLE

Share the breakdown

Each person gets a clearly listed share. Rename participants, mark payments, or copy a summary for your group chat.
